Privacy policy

Current for iPhone. Effective 29 September 2026.

The short version

Current does not collect any data. What you log stays on your iPhone. We cannot see it, and nobody else can see it through us.

No account, no network, no tracking

  • There is no account and no sign in.
  • The app makes no network connections. It does not send your entries, or anything about how you use the app, to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third party code or services in the app.

Where your data lives

Your entries are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app, using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). They are not synced to any server by Current.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include the app's data in that device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Encrypted backup (optional)

You can choose to create an encrypted backup file. The file is encrypted on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from a passphrase you choose. You decide where to save or share the file. We never receive it and we cannot recover your passphrase, so keep it somewhere safe.

CSV export (optional)

You can choose to export your entries as a CSV file. The export only happens when you ask for it, and you decide where the file goes. A CSV file is not encrypted, so treat it with care.

Face ID lock (optional)

If you turn on the lock, Current asks iOS to confirm it is you with Face ID or your passcode. This check is handled by iOS on your iPhone. Current never receives your face data.

Reminders (optional)

If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled as a local notification on your iPhone. No notification service or server is involved.

Widget and Control Center

The widget and the Control Center control show only simple status: how many of the last seven days you have logged, and whether today is logged. They do not show what you logged.

Children

Current is not directed at children, and it does not collect data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone. Any backup or export files you created are yours to keep or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
